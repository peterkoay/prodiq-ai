The problem

Product teams are drowning in signal.
Decisions default to loudest voice.

In complex multi-platform, multi-partner environments, product decisions are made under time pressure with incomplete context. The backlog grows. Sprint velocity drops. The wrong things ship first.

"We have 200 items in the backlog, three stakeholder groups with competing priorities, two platform vendors, and a migration deadline. The question isn't what to build — it's what to build next, and why, with evidence that holds up in a room full of people who disagree."
Priority drift

Without systematic signal analysis, sprint priorities shift based on recency bias and stakeholder volume — not subscriber impact or dependency risk.

Context overload

PMs synthesise JIRA, vendor release notes, platform logs, and partner SLAs manually. Critical connections between items go undetected until sprint review.

Decision debt

Deferred decisions accumulate. Teams enter planning with unresolved dependencies, blocker chains, and sprint commitments that don't survive first contact with engineering.

How it works

Five pillars. Six agents.
One product intelligence layer.

prodiq.ai embeds AI agents across five product management disciplines, orchestrated into a single decision-support pipeline — from signal ingestion to sprint-ready output.

PILLAR 01

Signal aggregation

Ingests backlog, JIRA epics, release notes, vendor advisories, and partner integration status. Structures fragmented input for AI analysis.

PILLAR 02

Dependency mapping

AI agents trace cross-item dependency chains — surfacing hidden blockers, migration gates, and sequencing constraints before sprint planning starts.

PILLAR 03

Priority recommendation

Evidence-backed prioritisation ranked by subscriber impact, dependency risk, and delivery confidence — not gut feel or stakeholder pressure.

PILLAR 04

Requirement framing

AI-assisted user story generation with acceptance criteria derived from signal data. Sprint-ready output that engineering can act on without three rounds of clarification.

PILLAR 05

Feedback integration

Sprint retrospective data, defect escapes, and velocity metrics feed back into the signal model — so the framework learns from every cycle.

Orchestration layer

All five pillars are coordinated by a six-agent orchestrator — a central AI layer that manages context handoff between agents, resolves conflicting signals, and produces a unified decision brief for each planning cycle. The orchestrator is what makes prodiq.ai a framework rather than a collection of prompts.

About

Built by a practitioner,
not a research project.

prodiq.ai is a self-initiated AI framework developed by Peter Koay, AVP of Product Management & Delivery at Astro Malaysia — under real resource constraints, on a live multi-vendor platform serving 5.3 million subscribers.

It was not designed in a lab or built for a slide deck. It runs alongside 2 embedded Linux STB platforms and 10+ OTT streaming partners, and has been validated across a few milestone release cycles since the beginning of 2026.

The framework is part of a broader AI-augmented delivery initiative that also includes buktiq.ai — a parallel framework for AI-augmented enterprise QA.
